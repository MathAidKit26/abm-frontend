DEFINITION

What is congruency?

Two figures or objects are congruent if they have the same shape and size regardless of their position and arrangement. ETC. Triangle that have same sides and angle are congruent

What is enlargement?

Enlargement is a transformation in which all the points of an object move from a fixed point with a constant ratio. The fixed point is known as the centre of enlargement and the constant ratio is known as the scale factor.

What is translation?

A translation is a geometric transformation that slides a figure from one position to another without rotating, flipping, or changing its size.

What is similarity?

Two objects are similar if they have the same shape, or one has the same shape as the mirror image of the other. ETC. All circles are similar to each other, all squares are similar to each other, and all equilateral triangles are similar to each other.
